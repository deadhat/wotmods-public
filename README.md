# Mods by OldSkool
v.2.4.0.0 - Public modifications used for my modpack.

### WOT 2.4
All mods are working.

### Mod descriptions:
* Advanced TechTree - Expands the tech tree to show all premium, collector's and hidden vehicles.
* Auto Ignore - Arty players are a piece of shit. This mod will blacklist them directly when they hit you.
* AVGRoll - Overlay showing your average damage per shell type.
* Battle Button - Allows you to change the battle button.
* Battle Efficiency - Shows a simple WN8 calculator next to your Players Panel.
* Contact Cleaner - Cleans up your contacts by hotkey: ALT + F12 clears the blacklist, ALT + F11 removes people who never accepted your friend request or who deleted you.
* Debug Panel - Decent grey debug panel with improved lag detector and clock.
* Destroyed Position - Keeps destroyed vehicles marked on the minimap.
* Friends Marker - Mark your friends during the battle in case you meet them.
* High Caliber - Shows how much damage is needed for high caliber archievement.
* Invites Manager - Lets you block or allow all kind of battle invites.
* KillShot Sound - Plays a sound of your choice whenever you destroy an enemy.
* Mastery in hangar - Shows the actual values needed for class 1 to 3 and mastery badge.
* Players Panel HP - Shows the ally and enemy HP in PlayersPanel on keypress or permanantly.
* SixthSense - Used in ProMod. You can use this to show your own SixthSense. Animations are supported.
* SmartTweaker - Little collections of different tweaks.
* SPGLog - Log the damage and stun taken from arty over an unlimited time.
* Spot Alert - Informs the team or platoon when spotted and remembers you when you should be unspotted.
* Spotted Status - Shows the state spotted and unspotted, neverspotted for enemys.
* Tank Panel - Shows enemy tank information on mousehover.
* TechTree Stats - Shows the gunmark progress and mastery badge (if archieved) in techtree.
* x5 Counter - Shows how many x5 XP vouchers you have left, next to the reserve count.
